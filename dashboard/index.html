        Server Builder

        Describe the change in plain words and the bot will make it. It can add or delete channels and roles, recolor roles, and set who can do what in a channel. It will never touch the bot's own setup channels and roles.

        Examples: "add a text channel called metal-memes" — "make a VIP role with a gold color" — "delete the off-topic channel" — "let the Member role send messages in general".
